## Help us understand data integration in Urban Digital Twins

Data integration is a fundamental requirement for developing effective Urban Digital Twins. However, integration can occur at different levels and through different technical mechanisms depending on the available data, system architecture, lifecycle phase, and intended urban application.

Our ongoing surveys aim to collect insights from researchers, practitioners, developers, public authorities, and other stakeholders involved in Urban Digital Twin initiatives.

The responses will contribute to a better understanding of:

- Data integration levels across the Urban Digital Twin lifecycle
- Technical mechanisms used to integrate heterogeneous urban data
- Current data integration challenges
- Interoperability and standardisation requirements
- Data integration practices across different Urban Digital Twin applications
