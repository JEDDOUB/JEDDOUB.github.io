## Overview

**City2Twin** is a lightweight and standards-based Urban Digital Twin platform developed at the GeoScITY laboratory, University of Liège.

The platform supports the integration, management, visualisation, and analysis of heterogeneous urban datasets, including:

- Semantic 3D city models
- Building energy data and simulation results
- Air-quality observations
- Sensor and time-series data
- Point clouds and geospatial datasets

City2Twin implements a three-tier architecture combining a CityJSON database, a Flask server, and an interactive web client. Its development emphasises openness, interoperability, transferability, reusability, and long-term maintainability.

The platform also supports analytical applications related to building energy assessment, air quality, and other urban decision-making use cases.
