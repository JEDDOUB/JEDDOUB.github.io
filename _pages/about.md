I am a **postdoctoral researcher** at the [Geospatial Data Science and City Information Modelling Laboratory (GeoScITY)](https://www.geoscity.uliege.be/), University of Liège, Belgium.

Originally from Rabat, Morocco, I have an academic background in geomatics and land surveying. My career has taken me from engineering and 3D geoinformation to research on the design and implementation of Urban Digital Twins.

My research focuses on **Urban Digital Twins, 3D city models, City Information Modelling, Building Information Modelling (BIM), multisource data integration, and geospatial interoperability**. I am particularly interested in the conceptual and technical challenges associated with integrating heterogeneous urban data throughout the Urban Digital Twin lifecycle.

My work bridges conceptual research and technical implementation, from developing conceptual frameworks and data integration approaches to designing databases, services, and interactive applications for Urban Digital Twins.

## Research background

I obtained my **PhD in Sciences in Geography and Geoinformation** from the University of Liège in 2026. My doctoral research investigated the design and technical implementation of different levels of data integration in Urban Digital Twins. It examined how heterogeneous urban data can be integrated throughout the complete lifecycle of an Urban Digital Twin.

My PhD was funded by the **Fonds de la Recherche Scientifique – FNRS (F.R.S.-FNRS)** as part of the PDR SIG3.0 research project and was conducted under the supervision of **Prof. Roland Billen**.

Since May 2026, I have continued my work at GeoScITY as a researcher, leading the technical development of the **City2Twin platform** and contributing to research and applied projects related to Urban Digital Twins, 3D city modelling, geoinformation, and human-centric urban applications.

## Projects and scientific activities

I am the technical lead of the **City2Twin Urban Digital Twin platform** and contribute to research and applied projects addressing urban applications, including **building energy, air quality, mobility, and human-centred urban planning**.

Alongside my research, I have contributed to teaching activities in **Urban GIS, FME, ArcGIS, and R&D in Geoinformation**, as well as to the co-supervision of master’s theses.

I also serve as a reviewer for international scientific journals, including *Building and Environment*, *Computers, Environment and Urban Systems*, *Cities*, and *Sustainable Energy Technologies and Assessments*.
