I am a **postdoctoral researcher** at the [Geospatial Data Science and City Information Modelling Laboratory (GeoScITY)](https://www.geoscity.uliege.be/), University of Liège, Belgium.

Originally from Rabat, Morocco, I have a multidisciplinary background in geomatics. My academic journey has taken me from land surveying, photogrammetry, and remote sensing to research in 3D geoinformation and its applications in urban environments.

My research focuses on **Urban Digital Twins, 3D city models, City Information Modelling, Building Information Modelling, multisource data integration, and geospatial interoperability**.

My work bridges conceptual research and technical implementation, from developing conceptual frameworks and data integration approaches to designing databases, services, and interactive applications for Urban Digital Twins.

## Current research

I lead the technical development of the **City2Twin platform** and contribute to research and applied projects addressing urban applications, including building energy, air quality, mobility, and human-centred urban planning.

[Learn more about my PhD research](/phd/) or explore my [projects and research resources](/portfolio/).
