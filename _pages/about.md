I am a **postdoctoral researcher in Geoinformation** at the [Geospatial Data Science and City Information Modelling Laboratory (GeoScITY)](https://www.geoscity.uliege.be/), University of Liège, Belgium.

My research focuses on **Urban Digital Twins, 3D city models, City Information Modelling, Building Information Modelling (BIM), multisource data integration, and geospatial interoperability**. I am particularly interested in the conceptual and technical challenges associated with integrating heterogeneous urban data throughout the Urban Digital Twin lifecycle.

## Research background

I obtained my PhD in Sciences from the University of Liège in 2026. My doctoral research investigated the design and technical implementation of different levels of data integration in Urban Digital Twins. The work examined how heterogeneous urban data, models, databases, services, and applications can be connected across the complete lifecycle of an Urban Digital Twin.

My PhD was funded by the **Fonds de la Recherche Scientifique (F.R.S.-FNRS)** as part of the PDR SIG3.0 research project and was conducted under the supervision of **Prof. Roland Billen**. The SIG3.0 project investigated the convergence of Geographic Information Systems with Web 3.0 technologies, including Semantic Web approaches, object-oriented web technologies, and document-oriented databases.

I joined GeoScITY at the University of Liège as a PhD researcher in March 2022. Since February 2026, I have continued my work at GeoScITY as a researcher, leading the development of the **City2Twin platform** and contributing to research and applied projects related to Urban Digital Twins and geoinformation.

Before joining the University of Liège, I completed a research internship at the **Helmholtz Institute Freiberg for Resource Technology (HIF-HZDR)** in Germany. My work focused on developing immersive virtual reality applications for visualizing hyperspectral point clouds and digital outcrops in mining environments.

## Projects and scientific activities

I am the scientific lead of the **City2Twin Urban Digital Twin platform** and contribute to research and applied projects involving 3D geoinformation, urban data integration, and digital twins.

Alongside my research, I have contributed to teaching activities in **Urban GIS, FME, ArcGIS, and R&D in Geoinformation**, as well as to the co-supervision of master's theses. I also serve as a reviewer for international scientific journals, including *Building and Environment*, *Computers, Environment and Urban Systems*, and *Sustainable Cities and Society*.
