This paper presents the Urban Digital Twin research conducted within the GeoScITY laboratory, focusing on architectural principles and tools that promote flexibility, accessibility, interoperability, and long-term maintainability.

The proposed approach combines semantic 3D city models, lightweight encodings, semantic validation mechanisms, and automated data integration workflows. It is illustrated through several tools developed at GeoScITY, including City2Twin, CERBERE, automated ETL workflows, and GIS4IoRT.
