This paper analyses how advances in data acquisition and artificial intelligence are transforming the generation and update of semantic 3D urban models.

It presents a complete workflow covering data acquisition and preprocessing, semantic segmentation, geometric reconstruction, and structuring into interoperable models. It also demonstrates how multi-temporal data can support the incremental update of 3D city models.
