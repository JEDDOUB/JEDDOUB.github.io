This paper introduces a human-centric framework for integrating subjective perception data into standardised 3D city models and Urban Digital Twins.

The methodology was tested at the Sart-Tilman campus of the University of Liège. Pedestrian routes were evaluated through a field survey addressing perceived conviviality and urban pollution. The resulting perception scores were processed and visualised through the UrbanSense web platform.
