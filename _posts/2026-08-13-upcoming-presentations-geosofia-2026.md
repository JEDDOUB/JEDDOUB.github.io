## Urban Digital Twins in Practice: A Survey-Based Analysis of Data Integration Levels and Approaches

**Conference:** 10th International Smart Data and Smart Cities Conference  
**Publication:** Full paper in the ISPRS Annals

This study examines how data integration levels are understood and applied by practitioners involved in Urban Digital Twin implementations.

Building on our conceptual framework, it investigates how integration approaches are selected across the Urban Digital Twin lifecycle, which tools support their implementation, and how data availability and characteristics influence these decisions.

The findings connect conceptual data integration levels with current practices and contribute to the development of more flexible and scalable Urban Digital Twins.

## Semantic Surface Integration in CityJSON-Based Urban Digital Twins: A Rooftop Photovoltaic Potential Use Case

**Conference:** 21st International 3D GeoInfo Conference  
**Publication:** Full paper in the ISPRS Annals

This work introduces a client-side workflow for integrating and visualising simulation results at the semantic surface level in CityJSON-based Urban Digital Twins.

The approach maps rooftop photovoltaic simulation results onto individual roof surfaces and verifies each assignment using geometric properties such as azimuth, tilt, and area. It also extends the open-source CityJSONThreeJS loader with a class-based visualisation mechanism.

The workflow is demonstrated within the **City2Twin platform** using 1,667 buildings from the Sart-Tilman district in Liège, Belgium.

I look forward to presenting these contributions and exchanging ideas with the international 3D geoinformation, smart cities, and Urban Digital Twin communities.
