This presentation introduced City2Twin as a lightweight framework for integrating, managing, visualising, and analysing heterogeneous data within Urban Digital Twins.

The presentation was delivered during the session **“Digital Planning: From Technocratic Myths to Sobering Realities”** at the inaugural BeNeLux Geography Conference.

**Authors:** Imane Jeddoub and Roland Billen.
