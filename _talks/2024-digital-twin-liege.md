This presentation described the creation of the core 3D city model for the Digital Twin of Liège using semantic segmentation and the automated modelling of LiDAR point clouds.

**Authors:** Imane Jeddoub, Zouhair Ballouch, Rafika Hajji, Jean-Paul Kasprzyk, and Roland Billen.

Imane Jeddoub and Zouhair Ballouch contributed equally to this work.
